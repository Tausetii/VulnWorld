About Us

VulnWorld started as a late-night idea between three friends who were tired of noisy bars and coffee shops that kicked you out the moment you plugged in a controller. We built a place where the volume knob goes up, the pizza stays hot, and strangers become squadmates.

Our story

We opened in a converted warehouse on Arcade Lane with a handful of restored cabinets and a dozen second-hand PCs. Word spread through local Discord servers and weekend pinball meetups. Today we’re still independently run: no franchise playbook, just staff who actually play the games on the floor.

Our kitchen runs parallel to the arcade—orders are tuned for quick turnaround so you’re not AFK from a tournament match for twenty minutes. From families with kids on racing sims to crews grinding ranked ladders, we try to keep the vibe welcoming and the rules simple.

What we care about

Fair play Clear rules, visible pricing, and staff who mediate disputes without drama.
Safety first Well-lit aisles, cable management we’re weirdly proud of, and allergen notes on the menu.
Community Local artists on the walls, charity streams in the LAN room, and mic etiquette that keeps voice chat usable.

Milestones

  • 2019 Signed the Arcade Lane lease with more optimism than capital.
  • 2020 First pinball league night—twelve players, one stuck tilt sensor, zero regrets.
  • 2022 Expanded PC bays and added the streaming nook for charity marathon weekends.
  • 2024 Kitchen renovation: bigger pass-through window so you never miss your queue pop.
